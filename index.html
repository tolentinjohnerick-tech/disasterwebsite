KNOW THE RISKS

Common Natural & Man-Made Disasters

Understand the dangers and learn how to respond.

🌪️
WEATHER

Typhoon

Strong winds and heavy rainfall can cause flooding and property damage.

🌍
GEOLOGICAL

Earthquake

Ground shaking can damage buildings. Remember: Drop, Cover, and Hold On.

🌊
WATER-RELATED

Flood

Rising water can be dangerous. Move to higher ground and avoid floodwater.

🔥
MAN-MADE

Fire

Fire spreads quickly. Know your exits and evacuate immediately.

📢 IMPORTANT REMINDER

Preparation is everyone's responsibility.

Make an emergency plan and share it with your family.

🏠
PREPARE AHEAD

Before a Disaster

Preparation can reduce risks and help protect lives.

📋
Be prepared before an emergency happens.

Take time to prepare your home, family, and emergency supplies.

🎒

Prepare an Emergency Kit

Prepare food, water, medicine, flashlight, batteries, and other essential supplies for at least three days.

📍

Know Evacuation Routes

Identify the nearest evacuation centers and safe routes in your community.

🏠

Secure Your Home

Secure heavy furniture, windows, and objects that may fall during an earthquake or strong winds.

📄

Protect Important Documents

Keep identification cards, certificates, and important documents in waterproof packaging.

👨‍👩‍👧

Create a Family Plan

Agree on emergency contacts, meeting places, and communication methods.

📱

Monitor Weather Updates

Follow official weather advisories and emergency announcements.

ACT IMMEDIATELY

During a Disaster

Your actions during an emergency can save lives.

⚠️
Stay calm and follow official instructions.

Prioritize your safety and the safety of the people around you.

🧘

Stay Calm

Avoid panic. Listen carefully to authorities and follow emergency instructions.

🌍

Earthquake Safety

Drop, Cover, and Hold On. Protect your head and stay away from windows.

🌪️

Typhoon Safety

Stay indoors and away from windows. Evacuate when authorities instruct you to do so.

🌊

Flood Safety

Move to higher ground. Never walk, swim, or drive through floodwater.

🔥

Fire Safety

Crawl low under smoke, cover your nose and mouth, and use the nearest safe exit.

🚪

Evacuate Safely

Use stairs instead of elevators and follow designated evacuation routes.

RECOVERY & SAFETY

After a Disaster

Recovery begins by ensuring everyone is safe.

💚
Safety does not end when the disaster is over.

Continue following safety precautions while recovering.

🩹

Check for Injuries

Check yourself and others for injuries. Give first aid if you are trained to do so.

🏚️

Inspect Your Home

Do not enter damaged buildings until authorities confirm they are safe.

💧

Ensure Safe Drinking Water

Use safe bottled or treated water. Follow public health advisories.

⚡

Avoid Electrical Hazards

Stay away from fallen power lines and report electrical hazards immediately.

📻

Listen to Authorities

Monitor official updates about relief operations, evacuation, and recovery.

🤝

Help Your Community

Help neighbors, especially children, elderly people, and persons with disabilities.
